The material

What is fiberglass composite?

Fine strands of glass are set in a hardened resin. The glass gives strength, the resin holds the shape, and together they make a shell that is light, strong and built for the outdoors.

Glass fibers

Thin, strong strands that reinforce the pot.

+

Resin

Binds the fibers and sets into a rigid shell.

=

Fiberglass

A light, tough planter with a protective finish.

Fiberglass composite is two ingredients working together. Glass fibers carry the strength; resin binds them into any shape. The finished pot gets a protective coating for color and weather protection.
